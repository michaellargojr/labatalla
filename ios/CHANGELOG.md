## 0.2 - Nov 11, 2026

<!-- Build 2. The rebuild to the full brief: sleep, reasons, goal setup questions, work and money, Insights, Head to head, the Oracle. Compiled and unit-tested on GitHub's simulator only. Not yet installed on a phone. -->

- Sleep: the first run asks how much sleep you need and when you want to go to bed and wake up. Each morning, a check-in that takes a few seconds: when you slept, when you woke, how long, how good, and the reason.
- Check-ins are now a question: did you work on it? Yes or no, how long, and the reason. One tap each. A no is kept with its reason and never shows on the calendar.
- Goals come in four kinds: ones that take hours, rules that take none, work, and results that other goals serve. Setting one up asks how important it is, three dates, times a week, total hours and how clear the path is, and does the sums for you.
- Work: tie a job to dollars and hours, however it pays. Each month the app asks for the month's number and shows what an hour has been worth against what it needs to be worth.
- The calendar shows each goal's hours for the week, the month and in all, against its plan, and a small mark for the night's sleep.
- Insights replaces Progress: the patterns in your sleep, hours, money and reasons, found without being asked.
- Head to head: if only one of two goals could happen, which? What you chose, next to what you said and what you did.
- The Oracle: ask Claude about the whole picture, with your own API key, or copy your brief and ask anywhere.
- Three optional reminders: the morning sleep check-in, the evening check-in, and the 1st of the month for a job. Tapping one opens the right screen.

## 0.1 - Nov 5, 2026

<!-- Build 1. The foundation, built in one session from the brief and Jimmy's calendar. Not yet installed on a phone. -->

- The calendar: Month and Week. Each goal has a color, and each day you do it gets a band of that color. Tap a day to see it and mark it.
- Goals: set what you are building, whether it matters in six months or six years, its color, its symbol, when, and why. Retire a goal and its days stay colored.
- Badges on a day: the star when every six-year goal asked of it was done, the bolt for every six-month goal, the crown for everything.
- Today, in the middle of the bar: today's list, one tap per goal.
- Progress: each goal's run, best run, total days and its last twelve weeks.
- Settings: dark or light, royal or tech blue, four app icons, Monday or Sunday weeks, haptics, export and import.
- An optional daily reminder, off until you turn it on: one quiet notification at the time you pick.
